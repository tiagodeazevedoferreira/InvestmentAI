# Task 024 — Provider calibration against historical outcomes

**Status:** IN PROGRESS

## Objective

Add a provider-neutral, deterministic calibration primitive that measures how external signal confidence relates to realized historical outcomes, without changing signal weights, thresholds, risk gates or execution authority.

## Scope

- preserve provider and horizon provenance;
- validate confidence and realized signed-return observations;
- calculate hit rate, mean confidence, Brier score, calibration gap and mean signed return;
- group results deterministically by provider and horizon;
- keep the result descriptive and research-only.

## Non-goals

- no automatic provider ranking or selection;
- no automatic weight/threshold changes;
- no signal-policy modification;
- no model promotion;
- no broker, MT5/DOTO or financial transaction;
- no profitability or production-readiness claim.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Valid provider/outcome observations produce a deterministic report. **PENDING**

4. Brier score, hit rate, calibration gap and mean signed return are calculated deterministically. **PENDING**
5. Empty observations fail closed. **PENDING**
6. The primitive does not modify weights, thresholds, risk gates or execution authority. **PENDING**
7. Backend tests are green. **PENDING**

9. Limitations and research-only interpretation are documented. **PENDING**

## Implementation

`ProviderOutcomeObservation` and `build_provider_calibration()` provide the offline statistical boundary. The calibration gap is defined as mean predicted confidence minus realized hit rate. Brier score uses the binary hit outcome as the realized target.

The primitive intentionally consumes already-attributed historical observations rather than querying a provider or broker. This keeps data acquisition, outcome attribution and calibration statistically separable.

## Interpretation boundary

A calibration gap is descriptive and does not establish that a provider is over- or under-confident in a statistically significant sense. Brier score is sensitive to the supplied confidence semantics and class definition. Sample size, selection effects, market regime and timestamp alignment remain material limitations.

No metric generated by this task authorizes promotion, provider ranking, weight changes or execution.

## Safety boundary

This task is research-only. It has no execution authority and does not contact brokers or financial venues.
